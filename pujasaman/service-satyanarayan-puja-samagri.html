Satyanarayan Puja Samagri List

A typical Satyanarayan Puja requires different items for the puja, offerings and prasad preparation. Common Satyanarayan Puja Samagri includes:

  • Roli, kumkum and haldi

  • Akshat (rice)

  • Mauli/Kalawa

  • Incense sticks and dhoop

  • Camphor

  • Cotton wicks

  • Ghee or oil

  • Diya

  • Kalash

  • Coconut

  • Mango leaves or other suitable leaves

  • Flowers and garland

  • Betel leaves and supari

  • Fruits

  • Panchamrit ingredients

  • Gangajal

  • Sandalwood/chandan

  • Tulsi leaves

  • Prasad ingredients such as wheat flour, sugar, ghee, banana and other ingredients as required

  • Havan samagri, if havan is included

The exact Satyanarayan Puja Samagri can differ according to your pandit's instructions, so it is always better to confirm the list before ordering.
