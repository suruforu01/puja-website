How to Book a Pandit for Home Puja

2 Sep 2026 · 5 min read

Pandit Ji se baat karte samay kin baaton ka dhyan rakhein. Yeh lekh ek samanya jankari hai; har parivaar ki parampara alag ho sakti hai, isliye antim list apne Pandit Ji se avashya confirm karein.

Zaroori samagri ki checklist

  • Puja ki tithi aur samay
  • Puja ka prakar
  • Pandit Ji ki dakshina ki baat pehle
  • Samagri ki list maangna
  • Parivaar ke sadasyon ki sankhya
  • Pata aur parking jankari

Taiyari ke liye kuch saral sujhav

Pehle se list banayein

Puja se ek-do din pehle samagri ki list Pandit Ji se lekar milaa lein, taaki last minute daud-bhaag na ho.

Taazi cheezein antim samay par

Phool aur fal jaise taaze items puja ke din ya ek din pehle mangwana aam taur par suvidhajanak rahta hai.

Aksar puche jane wale sawal

Pandit Ji kitne din pehle book karein?

Jitna jaldi ho sake; khaas tyoharon par thoda pehle sampark karna theek rahta hai.

Kya aap meri custom list ke anusar saman arrange karte hain?

Ji haan, aap list ki photo ya text WhatsApp par bhej sakte hain.
