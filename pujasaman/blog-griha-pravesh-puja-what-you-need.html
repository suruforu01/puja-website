Griha Pravesh Puja: What You Need

14 Sep 2026 · 7 min read

Naye ghar me puja ke liye samanya taiyari aur samagri. Yeh lekh ek samanya jankari hai; har parivaar ki parampara alag ho sakti hai, isliye antim list apne Pandit Ji se avashya confirm karein.

Zaroori samagri ki checklist

  • Kalash aur nariyal
  • Aam ke patte
  • Roli, kumkum, haldi
  • Havan kund aur samagri
  • Samidha
  • Navdhanya
  • Gangajal
  • Phool, fal, mithai

Taiyari ke liye kuch saral sujhav

Pehle se list banayein

Puja se ek-do din pehle samagri ki list Pandit Ji se lekar milaa lein, taaki last minute daud-bhaag na ho.

Taazi cheezein antim samay par

Phool aur fal jaise taaze items puja ke din ya ek din pehle mangwana aam taur par suvidhajanak rahta hai.

Aksar puche jane wale sawal

Kya Pandit Ji ki list alag hoti hai?

Haan, isliye apne Pandit Ji se list confirm kar lena achha rahta hai.

Kya aap meri custom list ke anusar saman arrange karte hain?

Ji haan, aap list ki photo ya text WhatsApp par bhej sakte hain.
