About PujaSamanNearMe

Puja Ki List Bhejo, Saman Ghar Pao

PujaSamanNearMe ek aisi service hai jo puja ki taiyari ko aasan banane ke liye shuru ki gayi.

Aksar puja ke din samagri ki lambi list lekar alag-alag dukanon par bhatakna padta hai.

Hamara maksad hai ki aap sirf Pandit Ji ki list bhejein aur baaki hum sambhal lein.

Aap list ki photo ya text WhatsApp par bhej sakte hain.

Hamari team list ko dhyan se padhkar har item ki availability check karti hai.

Phir item-wise price aapko bata di jati hai, taaki koi hidden charge na ho.

Fresh phool, fal, ghee, havan samagri jaise items ki quality par vishesh dhyan diya jata hai.

Saman ko leak-proof tareeke se pack kiya jata hai.

Delivery Dwarka, Janakpuri, Uttam Nagar aur aas-paas ke ilakon me ki jati hai.

Same day delivery selected locations par stock aur timing ke anusar available ho sakti hai.

Hum Satyanarayan, Griha Pravesh, Hawan, Rudra Abhishek, Navgrah aur Vastu Shanti jaisi pujaon ki samagri arrange karte hain.

Har parivaar ki parampara alag hoti hai, isliye hum Pandit Ji ki list ko hi pramukhta dete hain.

Hum kisi bhi puja ke natije ya fal ka daava nahi karte; hamara kaam sirf samagri uplabdh karana hai.

Customer support WhatsApp aur phone dono par uplabdh hai.

Agar aapke paas poori list nahi hai, to hum samanya list suggest kar sakte hain jise Pandit Ji se confirm kiya ja sake.

Hamara lakshya hai ki puja ka din tension-free aur shaant rahe.

Pricing transparent rakhi jati hai aur confirm hone ke baad hi order process hota hai.

Hum samay par pahunchne aur sahi saman dene ko apni zimmedari mante hain.

Aapka feedback hamari service ko behtar banane me madad karta hai.
